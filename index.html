Kävlingeån Flooding Comparison

Information about this website: This website is intended for residents in Revinge to compare satellite imagery from 2022 and 2024, showing how flooding may affect the surrounding area and nearby homes.
Right Side Image: Satellite imagery from 2022, showing the area before the flooding.
Left Side Image: Satellite imagery from 2024, showing the area after the flooding.
Sources: European Union, Copernicus Sentinel-2 data [2022,2024], OpenStreetMap

Satellite Image Comparison

Drag the slider to compare satellite imagery from 2022 and 2024. Add points, Lines, Polygons or Circles to mark flooded areas of the map. Use the dropdown menu to change geometry type.
Hold down the 'shift' key to activate freehand drawing. To remove the last point of a line or a polygon, press 'Undo'.
